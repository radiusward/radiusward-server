# ADR 0030: EAP-TTLS

Status: Accepted (2026-11-09). Phase 5, work package 5.8, milestone 4 ([plans/phase-5-auth.md](../plans/phase-5-auth.md)).

## Context
EAP-TTLS (RFC 5281) tunnels RADIUS-style attributes instead of a second EAP method. Its inner PAP lets a site keep
password hashes of any kind (or check them against a directory) while the password travels inside TLS; its inner
MS-CHAPv2 suits stores with NT hashes and Active Directory. Linux, Android and many supplicants offer it.

## Decision
- **On the TLS base** of ADR 0028/0029, without a client certificate. Inside the tunnel the client sends Diameter
  AVPs; radiusward reads User-Name and either User-Password (PAP; zero padding removed) or MS-CHAP-Challenge and
  MS-CHAP2-Response (MS-CHAPv2). A mandatory AVP it does not understand fails the conversation (§10.1).
- **PAP** is checked like PAP outside a tunnel — every stored format, or a credential verifier (LDAP bind, Netlogon) —
  and succeeds at once.
- **MS-CHAPv2** uses the implicit challenge (§11.1): 17 bytes `ttls challenge` from the tunnel, challenge and ident.
  A response to any other challenge is refused, so a challenge cannot be relayed from another session. Success sends
  MS-CHAP2-Success; the client's acknowledgement completes the conversation.
- **The inner user is the user**, as in PEAP: authorized again after success; the outer identity may be anonymous.
  Outages keep the conversation and repeat only the inner check on retransmission.
- **Keys**: `ttls keying material` (TLS 1.2), `EXPORTER_EAP_TLS_Key_Material` with type 21 (TLS 1.3, RFC 9427).
- With TLS 1.3 a client may send its AVPs with its Finished; when the server has nothing to send after the handshake
  it hands the turn to the client with an empty request.

## Consequences
- Inner CHAP, MS-CHAPv1 and inner EAP in TTLS are not implemented yet; PEAP's inner EAP-GTC and EAP-TLS come in
  milestone 4b, crypto-binding with milestone 5.
- Tested with a JDK TLS client and an inner peer deriving the challenge from its own session; `eapol_test` interop in
  milestone 5.

CHAP, MS-CHAPv1 and EAP inside TTLS followed in work package 5.16 ([ADR 0040](0040-ttls-inner-methods.md)).
